PRODUCT OVERVIEW
The ABB Type M2001 is a compact, multi-parameter water quality transmitter
designed for continuous inline monitoring in drinking water, wastewater, and
industrial process applications. It accepts up to 4 sensor inputs
simultaneously and provides Modbus RTU, HART 7, and 4-20 mA outputs.

Inputs:                Up to 4 x ABB analog/pH/ORP/DO/turbidity/temperature sensors
