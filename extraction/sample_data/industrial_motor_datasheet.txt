PRODUCT: SIMOTICS GP 1LE1 Series Three-Phase Induction Motor

1. GENERAL DESCRIPTION
The SIMOTICS GP 1LE1 is a versatile three-phase squirrel-cage induction motor
designed for general purpose industrial applications. It meets IEC 60034-1
standards and is suitable for direct-on-line or star-delta starting.
